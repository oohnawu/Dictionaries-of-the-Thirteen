# Data rights and licence boundaries

## Software

The root MIT licence applies to software for which the contributors hold the relevant rights. It does not apply to source dictionaries, dictionary excerpts, evaluation reference text, numerical datasets, model weights or third-party libraries merely because they are in or used by the repository.

## Evaluation datasets

The depositor confirmed on 13 November 2026 that the evaluation definitions, examples and five-shot demonstrations supplied for this release may be publicly redistributed. The packaged test files retain those supplied contents without modification.

The evaluation materials contain content derived from the Dictionaries of the Thirteen Classics (DTC) and the Hanyu Da Cidian (HDC). Rights in third-party dictionary material remain with the respective rights holders. No blanket Creative Commons, MIT or commercial re-use permission is granted for that material by this repository. Users requiring rights beyond the applicable permissions must obtain them from the relevant rights holders. Public accessibility must not be interpreted as permission to redistribute the entire underlying dictionaries.

## Excluded resources

Complete dictionary scans and OCR transcriptions, full structured dictionary records, fine-tuning corpora, retrieval indexes and raw retrieval evidence are not distributed. Obtain underlying materials through lawful channels. The authors do not promise to redistribute restricted source material on request.

## Numerical summaries and human evaluation

Included summaries are exported research statistics. Individual expert worksheets, free-text comments, identities and text-bearing rating manifests are excluded. The public summaries contain neither raw expert ratings nor simulated ratings. They permit inspection and plotting of the reported statistics, not independent recomputation of all human-evaluation statistics.
